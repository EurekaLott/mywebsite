HOW IT WORKS — From clicking Auto Run to matching partial numbers of the draw

The screenshots below are real, in order, from an actual run of the system for the Saturday, Aug 1, 2026 drawing — nothing staged.

STEP 1
  • Pick a program (e.g., EurekaLott Powerball 5) and press the ⚡ Powerball USA — Auto Run button.
  • The system automatically fetches 24 drawings in RAW order from the previous Saturday (July 25, 2026). Alternatively, you can paste 24 drawings yourself using the Other Lottery button if you want to try a sorted order. Some programs work better with Raw, others with Sorted. Experiment and discover what works best.
  • You only need to watch one number: the epoch counter. Once it reaches 12/12, training is complete.
  • Feel free to step away and come back later to retrieve your results and buy your tickets without waiting anxiously.
STEP 2

The system generates four core prediction numbers representing these two branches.

  • Left Branch: 69
  • Right Branch: 30

Success does not require all four numbers to appear at once. The model achieves victory as soon as even one number carrying the energy of 369 aligns precisely at the center.

'4 choose 1' Strategy: combining multiple EurekaLott outputs from Step 2 to Step 6 to pick your 6 ticket numbers is much easier than blindly guessing 6 out of a pool of 69. It is especially helpful when different EurekaLott programs generate the exact same number — such as 05 in Step 2 and Step 3, for example — which plays a crucial role in the customer's selection process.

STEP 3

If you click the Auto Run button, the program automatically checks the 3 checkpoints for you. If you use the Other Lottery button, check whether Monday, Wednesday and previous Saturday draws have already come out.

  • If they have not yet appeared → Today (Saturday) is the final time you can use this prediction.

In short: Previous Saturday data → Check Mon, Wed & Previous Sat → Final decision on current Saturday.

If the numbers have already come out, and instead of canceling you still decide to use them, that decision is up to you.
